Plain-language content pilot (v7)

New learners use the draft v10 lesson pack. This version rewrites the five lessons and practice instructions in simpler language. It keeps the same five concepts, question families, answer keys, and two teaching formats: worked text alone or the same text with a labeled diagram.

The comparison rules have a new version because wording can affect learning. Protocol v7 is prospective and uses content v10. Existing v6 assignments and v9 learner records stay on their original versions.

The assignment unit remains one concept for one learner. The fixed seed is 20261020. The primary held-out families, return windows, target study time, and minimum independent-learner threshold remain the same as v6. A learner's later answers are related observations from one person, not separate participants. Missing, assisted, late, and previously seen checks remain separate from eligible independent delayed outcomes.

The activity-choice rules are unchanged. Their explanations use simpler wording under recommendation policy pilot-v5; older content continues to use its prior policy text.

The app can show what a learner studied and answered. It cannot conclude that simpler language or a diagram caused better learning from synthetic tests or a single favorable result. Until valid real delayed evidence is available, the comparison remains insufficient_evidence.

The v10 pack and answer keys remain draft. An AI-assisted wording review does not establish qualified human content validation.
