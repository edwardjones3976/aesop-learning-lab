# Prospective five-concept descriptive protocol v5

Protocol `text-vs-labeled-diagram` v5 prospectively enrolls only new learners on content `m2-draft.8` (`v8`). Earlier assignments remain tied to v1–v4 and their original content packs. V5 corrects the fresh scored application-practice gap in the recursive-case concept; it does not reinterpret historical responses. The primary held-out family for each concept, seven-day target, days 7–14 analysis window, ten-minute suggested study target, and analysis rules remain as specified in [v4](descriptive-pilot-v4.md). The assignment seed is `20261021` and the assignment unit is one concept per learner. The seeded text/diagram allocation is persisted before instructional exposure and cannot change on restart.

The text and diagram arms share the worked text, objectives, examples, retrieval practice, feedback, and held-out assessments. The diagram arm adds only the labeled diagram. Exposure start and completion, encountered item-family IDs, condition, content/protocol versions, first answers, hints, reveals, outside-study responses where supplied, and actual delayed timing are retained. Elapsed opening time is not proof of attentive study.

The primary observation is the first unassisted, unfamiliar, deterministically scored day-seven held-out response within days 7–14 after exposure completion. Exposed families, assistance, outside study, invalid or ungraded answers, and answers outside the window remain recorded but are not eligible primary observations. Missingness is reported, never imputed. Candidate draft keys make matches provisional and ineligible until genuinely reviewed. Responses from one learner across concepts are dependent, and carryover or order may explain apparent arm differences. The 20-independent-learners-per-arm gate from v4 is unchanged; a personal descriptive report may still show observations but must return `insufficient_evidence` for an instructional effect. No automatic policy change follows a favorable result.

The software test journey can establish that records, scheduling, and classifications behave as specified. It cannot establish educational efficacy or replace qualified independent content review.
