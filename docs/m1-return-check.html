M1 return-visit observation

The automated journey uses an injected clock to verify that day-one and day-seven checks become due. It does not measure whether a learner remembers the material on those days.

For a real observation, use a separate local learner profile. Complete a lesson and an independent practice answer, then close the browser and service. Return at least 24 hours after that lesson without reviewing its answer first. Record the offered activity, the recommendation reason and policy version, the first answer, hint use, score status, and content version. Repeat at least seven days after the lesson for the distinct week check. Confirm each attempt remains after another service restart and that the earlier outcome was not rewritten.

The pack remains a draft with candidate answer keys and no independent receipt for m1-draft.3. Report the observed answer and assistance separately. Do not label either result mastery or a proven learning effect. Keep learner records local, and export or delete them through the preview controls when the learner chooses.
