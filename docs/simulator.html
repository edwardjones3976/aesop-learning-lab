Simulator (round 2)

One scenario-driven simulator for the frozen contrast (protocol v1), built in wave 1 of the round-2 decision (aesop-declutter-plan/simround2-decision.html). Simulation only: synthetic learners come from a model that is independent of the estimator, and results test whether the product misleads, never how people learn.

Commands

py scripts/sim.py list
py scripts/sim.py run round1-gate hard-learner --out DIR --workers 6 --parity 50
py scripts/sim.py parity hard-learner --learners 100
py scripts/sim_break_checks.py

Parts (src/aesop_lab/sim/)

  • scenario.py holds versioned scenario files (scenarios/*.json, schema sim-scenario/1) with a learner model, behaviour, design and fixed seeds. A file may be a suite of rows. Unknown fields are rejected, and every row has a hash.
  • learner.py holds the learner models:
    • bernoulli: round 1's model, a fixed recall per arm;
    • half-life: P = 2^(−Δ/h) · invlogit(intercept + θ − b − concept + δ);
    • log-delay: a mismatch check.
    It adds personal method effects with optional sign reversal and expertise drift.
  • Behaviour, in learner.py:
    • skipping when failure is expected, plus per-arm skipping;
    • outside study, declared or not, per arm;
    • key false negatives per arm;
    • late answers, missed days, dropout and frustration dropout.
    Unit order and block roles are computed exactly as the engine computes them.
  • Truth. Each learner's own causal gap is the mean of pvariant − pbaseline at day 7 over the units they studied. Behaviour does not enter it.
  • fastpath.py judges a trajectory without SQLite, mirroring MicroUnitEngine.contrast_status: cutoffs on days 14, 21 and 28 from the first exposure, the 7–14 day window, contamination, and protocol.judge.
  • replay.py replays the same trajectory through the real engine (an in-memory SQLite copy of a migrated template) and compares the outcome, decision day, winner, per-look counts, and probabilities within 1e-6.
  • run.py runs rows over one shared process pool and replays at least 50 engine learners per row. A row passes only if the one-sided 95% Wilson upper bound for wrong calls is below 5%. A wrong call is judged against each learner's own sign: a directional call for the arm that is worse for them, or "no meaningful difference" when |gap| ≥ 5 points.

Wave 1 results

  • Engine parity: 700 of 700 sampled learners across both suites, plus 100 of 100 on a 10,000-learner run.
  • round1-gate reproduces round 1 within its Wilson intervals (null: 12/500 wrong calls against round 1's 10).
  • Speed: 10,000 learners in one row with 100 parity replays took 65 s on 6 workers, against round 1's 4,000 learners in 22 minutes. p_diff_greater is now memoised; it is pure in its integer inputs.
  • Planted bugs: scripts/sim_break_checks.py catches 6 of 6: a leaked future outcome, a day-1 check counted as delayed, a swapped key, ignored outside study, broken block balance, and a wrong call judged by the population sign.
