One-day and one-week check contract

This extends the original scheduler contract for the m1-draft.3 development pack. The original v1 and v2 packs and learner records remain pinned to their versions. The new pack uses pilot-v2 in recommendations.

Each activity has check_delay_days: zero for practice, one or seven for a held-out check. The delay is measured from the persisted lesson completion time. The earlier check must have an attempt before the seven-day check becomes eligible. Its score and hint level do not determine eligibility, so a weak day-one result does not remove the opportunity to observe week-one retention. The seven-day item has a different, previously unattempted family. An attempted held-out item is not offered again.

An unassisted correct scored answer after lesson completion is still needed to open the first check. Practice remains available before and after either due time. A learner can choose practice when a check is due. nextCheckAt is the next eligible check time, which may already be past. It is not a claim that an attempt occurred or that a learner retained the concept.

delayedChecks records each attempted interval with checkDelayDays, assistance, score, and independentSuccess. delayedOutcomeByDay reports observed or insufficient_evidence for each interval separately. Both keys and instructional items remain unreviewed candidates; these observations are not mastery estimates.
