# Personal-learning pilot update

New local learner records use the five-concept `m2-draft.5` pack and the prospective `text-vs-labeled-diagram` protocol `v2`. Versioned v4 content and protocol v1 are retained for learners whose lesson, assignment, exposure, answer, draft, or override already references v4. A partial lesson view pins its original content and condition after restart. Deletion explicitly clears local records and starts a fresh v5 learner; export before deletion if the learner wants a copy. No historical outcomes are migrated into v2.

The current content remains **draft** and scored keys remain **candidate**. A non-author engineering review can identify technical defects and record a review disposition, but does not fabricate human subject-matter validation. The interface must continue to show this limitation and must not call a single immediate answer mastery. Free-form explanations and code designs remain ungraded pending rubric self-review or a qualified human review.

Protocol v2 fixes five day-seven item families, a seed, assignment method, and a shared ten-minute study target before new enrollment. The lesson offers the target to both conditions, but study remains self-paced and the app does not verify attentive exposure time. The primary observation is a first independent, unfamiliar, deterministically graded check between days seven and fourteen. Draft key matches are development observations and cannot enter the eligible primary outcome. One local learner may contribute several correlated concept observations but is at most one independent learner in each arm. Thus the local report remains `insufficient_evidence`; the policy-effect gate cannot automatically change instruction. Details and future multi-learner limits are in [the prospective protocol](descriptive-pilot-v2.md).

The scheduler records why it suggests practice or a due check. A hint-assisted answer leads toward a fresh independent first answer. Repeated misses on one item lead to another item and a *candidate* content-quality flag, not a diagnosis of either learner or question. Review timing, saved first answers, independent and assisted attempts, delayed results, missing dimensions, and overrides are visible on return.

For reproducible verification, use a separate temporary learner database and an injected clock. `tests/test_personal_pilot_journey.py` covers all five lessons, assisted versus independent outcomes, restart, override, and day-one/day-seven checks. A controlled-clock success verifies state transitions, not elapsed real days or educational effectiveness. A future real next-day/week check must use the same person's actual persisted record at the real due times and inspect the first answer, assistance, family, content version, and outside-study annotation without manufacturing a result.
